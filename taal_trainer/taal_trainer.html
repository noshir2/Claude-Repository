Taal Trainer

Counts the way a tabla player counts: theka bols matra by matra, with sam (X), taali (claps, 2 3 4) and khali (wave, 0). Works offline on this device only; nothing is uploaded.

Thekas vary by teacher and gharana; confirm with your teacher. Entries marked "confirm" are a common form; a teacher may use different bols or divisions. Edit the table at the top of this file.

No sound? Check the phone's silent switch and volume. The beat still runs on screen without sound.
