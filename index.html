Toolbox

These are small tools built for you, one for each thing you do in a normal week. Everything runs on the page in your browser, and nothing you type or choose is uploaded anywhere.

Your files, your trades, your roster, your photo: they stay on your own device. Close the page and they are gone, apart from a few settings a page remembers in this browser.

The tools

Options wheel

The wheel is the routine of selling a put, taking the shares if you are assigned, then selling covered calls on them. Two tools on one page: the results checker reads your Webull order history and tells you how much money your puts and covered calls have actually made, and the planner helps you decide what to sell next, whether a roll is worth it, and whether you have the cash for assignment this week.

You need: the order-history file (.csv) you download from Webull (for the results checker), or, for the planner, the numbers Webull shows on its screen for a put or call (the strike, the expiry date, the premium and the share price).Works on a phone: yes

Open the Options wheel page

Robotics electrical card

The wiring rules, reference numbers and calculators a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) electrical mentor needs on a shop night, plus flashcards and printable warm-ups for students.

You need: nothing.Works on a phone: yes

Open the Robotics electrical card

Fantasy football

Tells you who to start this week, whether a trade offer helps your starting lineup, and which waiver pickup adds the most points, using your own league's scoring.

You need: six weekly projection files (.csv), one per position, that you download from the FantasyPros website (a free site, separate from the Yahoo app), and your roster typed in once.Works on a phone: yes

Open the Fantasy football page

Taal trainer

A metronome that counts the way a tabla player counts: it shows the bols (the spoken syllables of the beat) beat by beat, marks the claps and the wave with different sounds, and can hide the bols so you recite them from memory.

You need: nothing. Turn the sound on and, on a phone, check the silent switch.Works on a phone: yes

Open the Taal trainer

PE continuing education

Keeps your list of continuing-education courses and tells you whether you have the hours your Illinois PE renewal needs, and what is still missing.

You need: your list of courses (date, hours, title), typed in on the page or chosen as a spreadsheet file (.csv).Works on a phone: yes

Open the PE continuing education page

Passport photo

Crops a photo to the exact size a form or passport office asks for, keeps it under the file-size limit, and can lay out a 4 x 6 inch print sheet. The photo never leaves your device.

You need: a photo.Works on a phone: yes

How to use these

Open this link on your PC or on your phone and tap a tool. The Options wheel, Fantasy football and PE continuing education pages each have a button called Try it with example data: press it first and the page fills itself in with made-up numbers, so you can see what a result looks like before you use your own. The Passport photo page starts with a button called Try it with a drawn face that does the same job, then Choose photo for your own picture. The Robotics card and the Taal trainer need nothing from you, so you can use them straight away.

At the top of every tool page there is a link called Back to the toolbox that brings you back here. The browser's Back arrow works too.

One limit of the online copy: it cannot save files to your device and it cannot print. For the photo tool, and for printing a warm-up or cheat sheet, use your own copy of the files instead (see below). The online copy still shows every result on the page, so you can read it, copy it, or save a picture by pressing and holding it (phone) or right-clicking it (PC).

Getting your own copy

The files live on a website called GitHub, which is a place that stores files. Downloading them needs no sign-in and no install. The files you get are exactly the same as the ones behind this link. Take the steps one at a time; none of them can go wrong in a way that matters, and you can start over at any point.

  1. Open the project page in your browser: github.com/noshir2/Claude-Repository. Tap that link, or type the address into the address bar at the top of the browser. The page is public, so no account or sign-in is needed.
  2. Near the top left of the project page, just under the project name, is a button that shows a name beginning with claude/. If it already says claude/lucid-dijkstra-gic3gd, go to the next step. If it shows a different name, press it, type lucid in the box that appears, and choose claude/lucid-dijkstra-gic3gd from the short list. The page reloads with that name on the button. (GitHub calls these names branches; a branch is one saved version of the files, and that one is the version this page describes.)
  3. Press the green Code button, on the right above the list of files, and choose Download ZIP. A file whose name ends in .zip lands in your Downloads folder.
  4. On Windows, right-click that ZIP file, choose Extract All, then Extract. You get an ordinary folder with the same name. On a Mac, double-click the ZIP file and the folder appears next to it.
  5. Open that folder. Inside it is one more folder, whose name starts with Claude-Repository. Open that one too. This is where the files are.
  6. Double-click index.html. If your computer asks which program to use, choose your web browser (Chrome or Edge). This same page opens, and every Open button now opens your own copy, which can save files and print.

On a phone, use the online link; it is the easier route. If you want the files on a phone anyway, the file called README.md inside the taal_trainer folder has a short section called Opening it on a phone. Open that file on your PC: right-click it, choose Open with, and pick Notepad.

Words you will see

.csv file
A spreadsheet saved as plain text; Excel opens it, and it is the kind of file Webull and FantasyPros give you when you download your data.
Export, download
Two words for the same thing: asking an app to give you a file of your own data so you can use it elsewhere.
Option
A contract about buying or selling 100 shares at a set price before a set date; selling one is how the puts and covered calls here earn money.
Put
A contract you sell that promises to buy 100 shares at the strike price if the buyer wants you to, in return for the premium.
Covered call
A contract you sell on shares you already own that promises to hand them over at the strike price if the buyer wants them.
Premium
The money you receive when you sell an option; it is yours to keep whatever happens next.
Strike and expiry
The strike is the share price the contract is tied to, and the expiry is the date the contract ends.
Assigned
What happens when the buyer uses the contract: a put makes you buy the shares, a covered call makes you sell them.
Roll
Buying back a contract you sold and selling a new one with a later date or a different strike, usually to avoid being assigned.
Projection
Someone's estimate of how many fantasy points a player will score this week; FantasyPros publishes them as files.
Roster
The list of players on your fantasy team, starters and bench together.
PDH
Professional development hour: one hour of approved continuing education, the unit your PE renewal counts.
Browser tab
One open page in Chrome or Safari. The tools open in the same tab as this page; use the Back to the toolbox link at the top of a tool, or the browser's Back arrow, to return here.
Pop-up, blocked download
A message from the browser saying it stopped a page from opening a window or saving a file; the online copy of these tools is not allowed to save files, so this is expected there.

If something looks wrong

  • Reload the page. That clears any half-finished state.
  • If the page has a button called Try it with example data, press it to see what a good result looks like, then compare it with yours.
  • A red box on the page tells you what to fix. It names the problem and the step to take.
  • Nothing you do on these pages can break anything. The tools only read your files; they never change them.
