PE continuing-education log

Keep a list of the courses you have taken and see at a glance whether your Illinois PE license renewal has the hours it needs and what is still missing.

Nothing you type or choose leaves your device. Close the page and your list is still here, because this browser keeps it on this device; nothing else is remembered.

This browser is not keeping your list (a private window, or site data is blocked). Your list will be gone when you close the page. Use "Copy as spreadsheet text" or "Download" below before you close it.

You already have courses in your list. Replace them with the 13 made-up example courses?

  1. Add each course you take in the form below: the date, the hours on the certificate, the title, who ran it, and which of the four kinds it is.
  2. Tick "I have saved the certificate" once the certificate of attendance is saved somewhere safe (a folder on your PC, or a cloud drive). If you save it later, press "Mark as saved" next to the course in your list. Illinois can ask for it up to 6 years later.
  3. Read the status card. It says how many hours you have for the current renewal, which of the three required hours are done, and how many days are left.
  4. Back up your list now and then with "Copy as spreadsheet text" or "Download", because the list lives only in this browser on this device.

        What the words mean

        PDH (professional development hour)
        One hour of continuing education. Illinois counts a course as one PDH when it has at least 50 minutes of instruction. Type the number printed on the certificate. If a certificate gives CEUs instead, one CEU is 10 PDH. For a college course Illinois counts 15 PDH per semester hour and 10 per quarter hour.
        Renewal period
        Illinois PE licenses expire on 30 November of every odd-numbered year. The courses that count for a renewal are the ones taken in the 24 months ending on that day. The page picks the period that contains today.
        Required hours (core hours)
        Every renewal needs at least one hour on Illinois rules for engineers, one hour on ethics or professional conduct, and one hour of sexual-harassment prevention training. They are part of the 30, not extra, and must be taken again each period.
        General (elective) hours
        Everything else that relates to engineering practice: 27 of the 30 hours.
        Carry-over
        Spare hours from the previous period that Illinois lets you bring into this one. Three conditions: taken between 1 June and 30 November of the previous renewal year; general (not required) hours; and beyond the 30 that period needed. At most 15. Write them on IDFPR form f2476 and keep it with your records.
        12 hours a day
        Illinois counts at most 12 PDH in one calendar day, midnight to midnight. If you log more, the extra does not count.
        Certificate
        The certificate of attendance the sponsor gives you. Illinois does not accept a bare list; in an audit it wants, for each course, the certificate plus the sponsor's name and address, the date and place, the hours and a short note of the subject. Keep them 6 years.

        What this can and cannot tell you

        • Illinois only. Other states have different periods, totals and required hours.
        • It does not know your licensure date. The first renewal after you were first licensed in Illinois is exempt from reporting hours; if that is you, the page still shows you as short.
        • It does not convert units. Type the PDH number the certificate states (see "What the words mean" for CEUs and college credit). More than 12 hours on one date trips the daily limit, so spread a long course across its real dates.
        • It cannot see your certificates; the tick only records that you say you saved one.
        • It does not know whether a course meets Illinois content standards, whether a self-study course had a graded exam, or the 8-hour limit on society and board service credit; that is on you when you type the hours.
        • Carry-over is an estimate from your list. IDFPR decides what it accepts, and you must file the form.
